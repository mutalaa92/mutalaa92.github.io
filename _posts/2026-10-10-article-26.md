font-family: &quot;Noto Nastaliq Urdu&quot;, serif; white-space: normal; color: rgb(0, 0, 0);">﴿<b><span class="arabic" dir="rtl" lang="ar" style="font-family: &quot;Noto Naskh Arabic&quot;, serif; direction: rtl;
